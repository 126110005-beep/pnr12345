Understand ticket status codes
CNF
Confirmed. Coach and berth details may appear after chart preparation.
RAC
Reservation Against Cancellation. Travel is permitted; berth allocation may change.
WL
Waitlisted. The ticket is not yet confirmed; check the current status before travel.
GNWL
General Wait List.
RLWL
Remote Location Wait List.
PQWL
Pooled Quota Wait List.
TQWL
Tatkal Quota Wait List.
CAN / MOD
Cancelled or modified passenger entry.

Meanings are general; use the live railway enquiry for the authoritative status.
